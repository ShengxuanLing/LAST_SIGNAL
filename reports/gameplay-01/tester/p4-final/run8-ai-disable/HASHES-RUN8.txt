## contract implemented (per review AI_DISABLE_TEST_REVIEW §5.2/§5.3)

  - per-frame, from the real stepFrame call: owner, aiEnabled, ctlPaused, mode, lastIntentOwner,
    the EXACT intent passed to the core (move/fire/aim/interact/dash), key flags, core queue TYPES,
    aiActionCount, takeoverCount, position/hp before-after, active scenes, and the real SimEvent[]
    returned by the tick;
  - pendingCommands is recorded by type only and NOT asserted empty (the spec keep-alive legally
    enqueues heal-player); only `teleport-player` would be a position write and it is asserted absent;
  - HP is NOT a precondition any more;
  - position attribution: writers = player-hurt(hit.position) | player-moved(position) in execution order;
    with >=1 writer the frame-end position must equal the LAST writer position (1e-6); with no writer
    the frame-end position must equal the frame-start position (1e-6, original precision).
    Unattributable movement fails; nothing is ignored.
  - window-level: aiActionCount constant, takeoverCount constant, lastAiAction null at both samples;
  - GameOver/LevelUp overlays are recorded and the window is marked NOT quiet (no stillness claim);
  - finally: restores the original stepFrame (fail-fast if it cannot) and writes
    OUT/manual-disable-step-trace.json even on timeout/assertion failure;
  - unchanged: 2500 ms window, 1500 ms pre-wait, 1e-6 precision, no extra tick, no sleep added,
    no clear/heal/teleport/freeze, no skip.
